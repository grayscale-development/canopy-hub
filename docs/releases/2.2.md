# Canopy Hub 2.2

**Milo, your Hub assistant**
- Milo now lives in a floating panel on every page. Drag it to any corner, or minimize it and pick up where you left off.
- Answers stream in as Milo writes them. You can stop an answer, ask follow-ups, and reopen earlier chats.
- Milo links the wiki pages it used, says plainly when the Hub doesn't cover a question, and knows which page you're on.
- Managers can paste or attach an email and ask Milo to update a wiki page. Milo shows the change first; nothing is saved until you approve, and you can undo it.
- Rate answers with a thumbs up or down to help us improve Milo.

**Wiki**
- Every page has a short, permanent link that keeps working if the page is renamed or moved.
- Tags are easier to use: global and section tags, typo-tolerant tag search, a warning when a similar tag already exists, and one place for managers to merge, rename and tidy tags.
- Type in the sidebar to filter pages instantly.
- Sections and groups show their pages as a table. Pages are wider, and their header actions are tidier.
- Managers get an Alerts button for pages that look identical or overlap, with a suggested fix they can apply or dismiss.

**Settings and feedback**
- Settings has a clearer layout behind the gear icon.
- Use Submit Feedback anywhere in the Hub. Managers review all feedback, including Milo ratings, in one inbox.

**Everywhere**
- Better phone layouts, clearer empty states and many small fixes.

**Behind the scenes**
- The release adds new database tables and columns for tags, permanent page links, Milo edits and feedback, alerts and saved settings. Existing content is kept.
- Milo reads attached `.eml` email files with a small new library, postal-mime.
